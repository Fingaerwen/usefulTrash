usefulTrash privacy policy

Effective 13 October 2026.

usefulTrash is a personal gym tracker (an Android app and a companion web page, the "PC view") made by Fingaerwen for their own use. Sign-ups are closed: only the owner's account can sign in. This page explains what data the app handles, including data it receives from Google.

Data the app handles

  • What you enter: your training plan, workouts, body weight and notes.
  • From Google when you sign in: your email address, name, profile picture and Google account ID.
  • Sign-in tokens that keep you signed in.

How Google user data is used

  • Sign in with Google (your email, name and profile picture): Google's sign-in token is passed to Supabase, the app's sign-in and database service, to sign you in to your own account. It's used for nothing else.
  • Google Drive (only "files this app created"): the app saves backup copies of your own app data to your Google Drive and deletes its own older backups (it keeps the last 3). It can't see, open or change any other file in your Drive. On the phone, Google Play services keeps this permission; the app only receives access tokens that expire after about an hour, keeps them in memory, and sends them only to Google. The PC view asks Google for Drive access each time you press its backup button.

Where data is stored

  • On your phone: your data, in the app's private storage. Sign-in tokens are encrypted with a key kept in the phone's security hardware (Android Keystore) and are left out of Android's backups.
  • In Supabase (database hosting): your data and your account record (the Google details above). Row-level security lets only your own signed-in account read or change your data.
  • In your Google Drive: the backup files the app created.
  • The PC view keeps its sign-in in the browser tab's memory only: closing the tab signs you out. It stores nothing in your browser.

Sharing

No data is sold, rented or shared with anyone. There are no ads, analytics or trackers. The only services involved are Supabase (sign-in and database) and Google (sign-in and Drive), and only to provide the features above.

Limited Use

usefulTrash's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices and deletion

  • Remove usefulTrash's access to your Google account at any time in your Google Account: Your connections to third-party apps & services.
  • Backups are ordinary files in your Google Drive: delete them there whenever you like.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes the data stored on your phone.
  • To have your account and data in Supabase deleted, open an issue on the project's GitHub page (below).

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/Fingaerwen/usefulTrash/issues.

Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new effective date.
