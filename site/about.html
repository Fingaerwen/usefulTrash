usefulTrash

usefulTrash is a pair of personal apps, made by Fingaerwen for their own use and still in development: Talon, a gym tracker (called Gym Tracker until version 0.4), and Ambrosia, a diet tracker. Each is an Android app with a companion web page, its "PC view". Sign-ups are closed: only the owner's account can sign in.

What they do

  • Talon follows a training plan: today's workout with sets, reps and weights, and rest days. It logs workouts and body weight, and shows progress, personal records and a weekly summary.
  • Ambrosia counts calories, protein, fat and carbs against daily targets. Food comes in by scanning its barcode or searching food databases, and a bulk check suggests changes to the targets from the weigh-ins in Talon.
  • Both work offline and sync when the phone is back online.
  • Each backs up its own data to the owner's own Google Drive.

Google data they use

  • Sign in with Google: only to sign in to the owner's account.
  • Google Drive: only the files the apps create themselves (their backups). They can't see or open any other Drive file.

Details are in the privacy policy.
