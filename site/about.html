usefulTrash

usefulTrash is a personal gym tracker, made by Fingaerwen for their own use and still in development. It's an Android app with a companion web page, the "PC view". Sign-ups are closed: only the owner's account can sign in.

What it does

  • Follows a training plan: today's workout with sets, reps and weights, and rest days.
  • Logs workouts and body weight, and shows progress, personal records and a weekly summary.
  • Works offline at the gym and syncs when the phone is back online.
  • Backs up its data to the owner's own Google Drive.

Google data it uses

  • Sign in with Google: only to sign in to the owner's account.
  • Google Drive: only the files the app creates itself (its backups). It can't see or open any other Drive file.

Details are in the privacy policy.
